This document presents version 2 of a community LaTeX template for academic works at the Federal University of Ceará, built on the ufctex class and the abntexto infrastructure. The work aims to provide a reproducible editorial foundation for undergraduate final projects, specialization monographs, master's dissertations, doctoral theses, and research projects while preserving institutional requirements that are compatible with current standards of the Brazilian Association of Technical Standards. The implementation organizes metadata, pre-textual elements, section-based textual structure, citations, references, illustrations, tables, framed tables, source code, algorithms, and post-textual elements through public interfaces and optional modules. Validation combines semantic and geometric PDF tests, a document-profile matrix, compilation with pdfLaTeX and LuaLaTeX, Biber processing, glossaries, indexing, and compatibility checks. For institutional deposit workflows, the reference document declares PDF/A-2b conformance and the release validation uses veraPDF. The test suite provides a consistent workflow for producing, reviewing, and maintaining the template without replacing the final verification of requirements established by the applicable course, graduate program, or selection notice.
