## Why R1 was reopened

The exact-main candidate `ab641d49c5f6a79ff54a16a78d1946a6e008fe6d` passed the automated R5 matrix, but explicit maintainer visual review found four user-visible defects in the canonical TCC. Automated success therefore did not satisfy R6 and that candidate is superseded for final publication.

The release-blocking findings are:

1. the approval line rendered `Aprovada em: dia de mês de 2026` instead of a complete day/month date;
2. the canonical approval committee rendered six members and department/center/unit lines, while the requested canonical example is three members only (advisor + two examiners) without those unit lines;
3. unnumbered/post-textual table-of-contents entries showed an artificial standalone period immediately after the title before the regular dotted leaders;
4. the canonical TCC printed an `ÍNDICE REMISSIVO`, which the maintainer requested to remove from this canonical work.

## Root causes

- The approval date and extended committee were literal values in `template/main.tex`; this was not a renderer ambiguity.
- `tests/integration/reference-corpus.sh` incorrectly treated fifth/sixth committee members as required canonical corpus markers, so the regression protected the wrong example.
- `abntexto-ufc/layout.def` deliberately forced one visible dot using `\hbox to 1.1em{\leaders\ufctocdot\hfil}` before the normal leaders. BBox inspection of the certified PDF showed that forced cell as the isolated period after titles such as REFERÊNCIAS and GLOSSÁRIO.
- The canonical source enabled `index=imakeidx`, carried `\index{...}` markers and explicitly called `\ufcPrintIndex`.

## Implemented correction

Canonical approval:
- `approval-date = {11 de setembro de 2026}`;
- exactly three visible committee members: advisor, examiner 2 and examiner 3;
- advisor/examiner unit fields blank in the canonical example;
- institution lines remain available; department/center/unit placeholder lines are removed.

Canonical table of contents:
- remove the forced initial 1.1em dot cell from `\extdotleaders`;
- retain normal dotted leaders only in space actually available;
- numbered textual entries continue to require dotted leaders;
- regression explicitly forbids reintroducing the old forced-dot construct.

Canonical index:
- `index = none`;
- no `\ufcPrintIndex` in `template/main.tex`;
- no `\index{...}` markers in canonical chapters;
- optional class support remains documented and independently covered by index/glossary fixtures.
