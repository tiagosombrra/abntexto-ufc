## R1.3 preservation constraint

R1.3 must not replace the guide wholesale. It may reorganize or extend prose only when tied to a matrix finding. Existing reviewed guidance is retained unless a contradiction, duplication, stale statement or demonstrated defect is recorded.

Every new release-required teaching guarantee must identify:

1. authority/classification;
2. expected rendered behavior;
3. public project/API mechanism;
4. validation/evidence route.

The full machine normative contract remains the atomic source of proof semantics. The pedagogical TCC summarizes major user-visible requirements; it does not duplicate all 199 atomic rules as prose.

## Intentionally separate or conditional material

The following are not duplicated as complete sections/examples inside the undergraduate TCC:

- scientific article: separate `template/scientific-article.tex` and article gates;
- research project and anonymized research project: profile-specific fixtures/gates with a cross-reference from the TCC;
- literal Times New Roman/Arial certification: Windows-specific evidence; public bundles do not redistribute Microsoft fonts;
- physical spine: conditional physical-production requirement, not forced into the electronic reference PDF.

These exclusions are deliberate scope boundaries, not missing coverage. The TCC must still explain their applicability and where the corresponding example/evidence lives.

## R1.2 closure decision

R1.2 is DONE because:

- a topic-by-topic baseline matrix exists;
- missing/partial content is explicitly enumerated for R1.3;
- strong existing material is protected from wholesale replacement;
- historical comparison proves the seven reviewed chapter bodies survived to the R1.3 boundary;
- intentionally separate/conditional scopes are identified;
- the machine state and continuation handoff point to R1.3 as the only next R1 action.

The initial matrix remains a **pre-R1.3 baseline snapshot**. Its rows intentionally continue to show what was missing/partial at inventory time; progress against those rows is recorded by R1.3 evidence rather than rewriting the historical baseline.
